# StoreScout

Help a retail expansion planner explore locations, test business conditions and select a store portfolio within a budget. The intended demo is Bengaluru grocery expansion with ten mapped candidate locations, assumed store economics and four fictional existing stores.

The landing explains this sequence and links into the working app. The app preserves saved scenario inputs, evaluates through the backend, compares two locations, displays POI-derived time-of-day opportunity, tests future infrastructure assumptions and produces a backend-generated investment plan and report.

The connected engine uses the delivered CatBoost reference-sales model trained on synthetic targets, followed by full-network allocation and deterministic cash flows. Real-store accuracy is not validated. Real online basemap streets do not validate synthetic businesses. Distinguish synthetic data, illustrative marketing scores and saved backend estimates. Never invent financial outputs or turn missing evidence into zero.

The interface should be legible and approachable: large direct headings, concise explanations, prominent next actions, readable charts, progressive disclosure for technical detail and one consistent retail visual identity. Preserve native controls, keyboard access, visible focus, pause/reduced motion, stale-result protection and scenario/evaluation identity.

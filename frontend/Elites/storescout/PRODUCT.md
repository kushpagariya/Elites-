# StoreScout
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User-specified Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, React Three Fiber and drei. Motion for scroll values.
## Users
Retail expansion managers, finance analysts and investment approvers.
## Product Purpose
Explain where new stores create incremental network value, what makes a site fail, and which candidates fit a capital budget.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Landing page only. /app is an explicit placeholder. PRD capabilities are proposed, not verified product outcomes. Six core capabilities and two differentiators: DaypartDNA and FailFast. Single-city prototype planned within a 36-hour build window. No invented accuracy, customers, testimonials or financial performance.
## Brand Commitments
StoreScout. White, black, one purple accent and tints. One persistent stylised hex city. No gradients, glass, neon, particles or stock art.
## Evidence on Hand
User master prompt and StoreScout_PRD.pdf. Illustrative geometry only; no live data or trained model in this marketing build.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic content, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, readable alternatives to every visual, mobile and no-WebGL paths.
